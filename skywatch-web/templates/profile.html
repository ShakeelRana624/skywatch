About The Project

SKYWATCH is an AI-powered smart city surveillance system that monitors live CCTV feeds to detect weapons, suspicious activities, and fire incidents in real-time. Upon detection, the system automatically generates alerts with location details and sends them to the nearest IoV (Internet of Vehicles) units for immediate response and action.
